# Internal Pilot Governance

## Oversight Committee
The internal pilot is overseen by a Clinical Governance Board comprising lead therapists, a safety engineer, and a compliance officer.

## Monitoring and Review
- **Weekly Review**: Audit logs are reviewed weekly for anomalies, blocked requests, and uncertainty flags.
- **Incident Response**: Any reported safety incident triggers an immediate review and potential deactivation of the feature flag.
- **Feedback Loop**: Therapists are required to submit qualitative feedback on system performance and safety every two weeks.

## Release Management
- Changes to the system during the pilot require approval from the Governance Board.
- Deployments must follow the established fallback and rollback procedures.
