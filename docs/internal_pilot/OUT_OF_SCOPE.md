# Out of Scope for Internal Pilot

The following areas and capabilities are strictly out of scope for the internal pilot:

- **Live Patient Data**: Integration with electronic health records (EHR) or intake of real, identifiable patient data.
- **Direct Patient Interaction**: Any interface or communication channel accessible by patients.
- **Automated Diagnosis**: The system providing diagnostic codes or definitive diagnoses.
- **Prescription and Medication Management**: Advice or automation related to pharmacotherapy.
- **Crisis Intervention**: The system handling or triaging active crisis situations.
- **Permanent Writes**: The system autonomously updating the production knowledge base without human curation.
- **Non-Clinical Advice**: Financial, legal, or administrative advice.
