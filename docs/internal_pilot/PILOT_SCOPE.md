# Internal Pilot Scope

## Overview
This document defines the scope of the internal therapist pilot. 

## In Scope
- Internal therapists accessing the system via approved, secure channels.
- Use of synthetic or strictly anonymized clinical case data.
- Queries regarding clinical guidelines, evidence-based practices, and general therapeutic strategies.
- Summarization of uploaded, anonymized session notes.
- Advisory outputs that are explicitly marked as requiring human review.

## Out of Scope
- See `OUT_OF_SCOPE.md` for detailed exclusions.
